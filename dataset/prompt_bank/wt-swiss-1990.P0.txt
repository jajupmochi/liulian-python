River water temperature is a key indicator for aquatic ecosystems, cooling-water use and climate impact assessment. This dataset contains daily mean water temperature in degrees Celsius from 28 hydrometric stations of the Swiss federal monitoring network (BAFU/FOEN) on the Rhein and Rhone river systems, recorded continuously since 1990. Each series shows a strong annual cycle between roughly 2 and 25 degrees Celsius and a slow warming trend.
