River water temperature is a key indicator for aquatic ecosystems, cooling-water use and climate impact assessment. This dataset contains daily mean water temperature in degrees Celsius from 63 hydrometric stations of the Swiss federal monitoring network (BAFU/FOEN) across Swiss river basins, covering the years 2005 to 2017. Each series shows a strong annual cycle between roughly 2 and 25 degrees Celsius. Water temperature follows air temperature with a damped seasonal cycle; alpine snowmelt lowers early-summer temperatures, lake outflows smooth short-term variability, and the long-term trend is about +0.27 degrees Celsius per decade.
