River water temperature is a key indicator for aquatic ecosystems, cooling-water use and climate impact assessment. This dataset contains daily mean water temperature in degrees Celsius from 15 stations of the canton Zurich river monitoring network in Switzerland, covering the years 2009 to 2019. Each series shows a strong annual cycle; water temperature follows air temperature with a damped seasonal cycle, lake outflows smooth short-term variability, and the long-term trend is warming by roughly a quarter degree Celsius per decade.
